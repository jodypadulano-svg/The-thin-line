## Prompt per Mage

### 01 · Riunione di redazione

> Monday morning in the supplied newsroom, at a modest editorial meeting table. Medium wide shot from the corner of the table. Sara in a pale blue work shirt smooths the fold of Friday's newspaper beside her open notebook. Across from her Barbara in a dark tailored outfit has a hand on the page, addressing the table. A couple of softly suggested colleagues at the edges. Cool office daylight with warm tones in the paper. Sara's attention is on the newspaper and her notebook.

### 01a · Sara prende la parola

> Edit 01. Preserve seats, clothes, notebook and newspaper. Sara has lifted her eyes to Barbara and is halfway through explaining her follow-up idea. One hand rests flat beside the notebook. Barbara looks up from the paper with a small, surprised smile. Move in slightly so both faces and Sara's open notebook remain in frame.

### 02 · Il tappetino occupato

> Early Monday evening at the supplied gym mat area. At mat height, frame adult Joanie sitting cheekily on the corner of an otherwise empty mat, one knee raised, in a sage workout shirt. Sara has just arrived next to her in slate-blue gym clothes, bag on one shoulder and a fond, amused look. Keep the second mat visible, with soft overhead light and muted evening color at the windows.

### 03 · L'ultima serie con i manubri

> Move the same Sara and Joanie into the supplied weight room. Sara is on a low flat bench doing a dumbbell press, two light dumbbells above her shoulders, both feet planted. Adult trainer Simon in a charcoal gym shirt crouches beside the bench and demonstrates the angle with his own arm. Joanie stands near the foot of the bench with a towel. Three-quarter side view, all three faces readable, overhead gym lights, believable equipment and exercise form.

### 03a · L'asciugamano

> Edit 03 after the set. Both dumbbells are safely down. Sara sits at the edge of the bench, amused. Joanie has just thrown her soft towel toward Simon and he catches it with both hands in front of him. Preserve their positions and the bench axis as much as possible. Catch the timing of an ordinary joke among friends.

### 03b · Sara chiede un'altra ripetizione

> Edit 03. Sara is focused on the end of a controlled dumbbell repetition while Simon counts beside the bench. Joanie stands close enough to help, supporting each light dumbbell from underneath as Sara finishes and they lower the weights safely. Clear hand placement, candid side angle, Sara looking toward the weights.

### 03c · La proposta di Joanie

> Reframe 03 toward the cable machine and full gym mirror. Joanie has just turned the bench toward the cables and gestures lightly between Sara and the machine. Simon has turned to face Sara and is waiting for her answer. Sara is upright with her arms relaxed. Keep the three in an eye-level medium shot with the bench and handles identifiable. Preserve faces and gym clothes.

### 03d · Prima ripetizione ai cavi

> Edit 03c. Use an oblique view into the wall mirror so the reflection shows the faces and the direct view establishes the cable station. Sara performs a standing cable fly with light resistance, feet staggered, elbows softly bent, hands moving together in front of her chest. Simon lightly guides the underside of each elbow with two fingers. In the mirror his gaze has dropped toward Sara's chest, and she sees him looking. Joanie watches from the side, fingers still on the cap of her water bottle. Keep the slate-blue top and sports bra in place. Frame the three faces, handles and the geometry of the exercise together.

### 03e · Ripetizione extra

> Edit 03d with the same mirror angle, clothing, cables and time of day. Simon has stepped back and his hands are clear. Sara deliberately wears the neckline of the same slate-blue workout top a little lower, showing visibly more cleavage with the sports bra still in place. At the close of the rep, she keeps her eyes on Simon in the mirror as he looks at her chest. Joanie raises the bottle to her lips and pauses before drinking. Keep faces and eye lines legible. Fully clothed, with the tension carried by the decision, the expression and the reflection.

### 03f · Gym squad

> Continue from 02 beside the mat area after training. Joanie holds her phone in one hand and types with her thumb while Sara leans in and Simon stands just behind them with his towel. Warm, casual three-shot, gym bags low in frame, equipment and mirrors soft in the background. The screen is turned away from the viewer.

### 04 · Il manoscritto

> Late Monday night in the supplied living room. A small writing table holds Sara's open laptop, a pencil, a shaded warm lamp and a thick cream business card propped against the lamp base. Sara wears a soft cream top and has turned partway from the keyboard. Josh in glasses and simple home clothes stands near the doorway, setting a mug down with the handle facing Sara. Medium two-shot from the far end of the table. Soft amber desk light meets the cooler, dim hallway.

### 04a · Sara difende la sua privacy

> Edit 04. Sara has turned fully toward Josh, still seated but straight-backed, her face intent, one hand resting near her laptop. Josh stops with his fingertips on the back of the empty chair. His expression is uneasy and defensive. Leave space between them and retain the untouched mug in its original position. Keep the lamp and doorway on the same sides.

### 04b · Sara lascia correre

> Edit 04. Josh is behind Sara, placing one hand gently on her shoulder before going to bed. Sara turns her eyes back to the laptop. Her nearer hand has lifted a little from the table, then stopped. Medium side angle includes both faces, the contact at her shoulder and the same mug and cream card. Warm lamplight, subdued expressions.

### 05 · Il messaggio a Daniel

> From 04, with Josh gone and the door closed. Sara alone at the writing table, holding her phone low over the tabletop. The phone gives a small cool reflection on her face as she reads, alert and still flushed from the argument. Keep the tilted laptop, untouched mug and cream card visible. Angle the phone display away from the camera for the game's chat overlay.

### 06 · Cancellare e riscrivere

> Edit 04 with the room quiet and Josh absent. Medium over-the-shoulder view of Sara returning both hands to the laptop, focus on the pause just before she deletes a selected paragraph. Suggest the selection with a faint slate-blue block and blurred lines. Leave the pencil and cream card beside the same warm lamp. Include enough of her profile to show concentration.

### 06a · Il romanzo, prima

> An illustration from Sara's novel. Use the established adult heroine from the door scene. Compose from the open terrace doorway at night, the adult stranger partly visible outdoors as the heroine crosses inside and takes her fiancé's hand. Their joined hands sit low in the frame, her face relieved and thoughtful. Use the villa terrace only as a spatial reference. Give the novel its own ink-blue palette, visible monochrome pencil hatching, pale amber interior, softened corners and washed paper.

### 06b · Il romanzo, dopo

> Edit 06a, preserving the fictional heroine, her clothing, both men and the architecture. She stays outside on the terrace and initiates a kiss with the adult stranger by taking his face in both hands. Through the glass, the fiancé is visible farther inside, laughing with his head tipped back, unaware. Medium wide side view with the glass dividing the terrace from the room. Retain the ink-blue and amber visual language of the novel. The kiss is brief and fully clothed.

### 07 · Le pagine inviate

> Continue from 06. Sara has sent her manuscript and is opening the sent copy. Side medium close-up in the pool of the same lamplight. Her hand rests lightly on the trackpad, her shoulders ease and a small private smile appears as she looks at the screen. The cream card and untouched mug are still beside her. The hallway is dark. Keep the screen soft for any text added in the game.
