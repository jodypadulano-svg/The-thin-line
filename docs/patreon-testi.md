## About (presentazione)

```
Hi, I'm Laura, and this is Fatal Conversion.

I've played a lot of visual novels. Now I'm making one: The Thin Line.

Sara is a young journalist with a novel in a drawer and a boyfriend she loves. She is happy, truly happy. Then, one day at a time, people start to notice her: her trainer at the gym, her boyfriend's arrogant boss, the lonely neighbour next door, her outrageous boss at the newspaper, a powerful publisher, her ex... and her best friend, who seems to know more than she says.

The Thin Line is a slow-burn adult visual novel about the line between who Sara is and who she could become. Every choice moves it a little. There are no bad endings, only different kinds of happiness.

WHAT TO EXPECT
- A story-first visual novel made in Ren'Py, with real choices and visible stats (Corruption, Confidence, Suspicion and each character's interest).
- Eight different routes, each with its own pace and its own side of Sara.
- A hand-painted watercolor-and-pencil look: every scene is a full illustration, no sprites.
- Slow burn. It takes its time, and the payoff is worth it.

STATUS
Episode 1 (13 in-game days, about 1.5 hours) is almost finished. Episode 2 is in progress. Episode 1 will be free for everyone; patrons get new episodes first.

18+ ONLY. All characters are adults. All intimate content is fully consensual.

Illustrations are created with AI tools and refined by hand; music is generated with Mureka. Writing, story and game design are mine.

Thank you for being here. Follow for free to get the news, and see where the line takes her.
```
